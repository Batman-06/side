TOPIC: The Eiffel Tower

- Located on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France.
- Designed by the engineering company of Gustave Eiffel.
- Built for the 1889 World's Fair (Exposition Universelle).
- Height: 330 meters (including antennas).
- Was the tallest man-made structure in the world until 1930.
- Made of wrought iron; weighs about 10,100 tonnes.
- Painted roughly every 7 years to protect it from rust.
- Receives about 7 million visitors per year.

TOPIC: The Great Wall of China

- Over 20,000 km long across northern China.
- Built and rebuilt over many dynasties, mainly Ming (1368-1644).
- Made of stone, brick, tamped earth, and wood.
- Built for border defense against nomadic groups.
